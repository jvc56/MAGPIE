What the provenance means

The static anchor contains the top 25 plays by static equity. Other groups contain plays outside that anchor, labeled by every check that admitted them. Overlapping checks share a group; an admission label is not proof of why the play succeeds. Raw blocking/setup labels are reconstructed from exported check scores only when they reproduce recorded membership; mixed Raw signals labels remain where rounding hides tie order.

Adjustment columns show equity points added to static equity by the wide checks: blocking = 2 × (exported threat value − static equity), setup = 3 × (exported setup value − static equity). A smaller blocking penalty ranks better; a larger setup bonus ranks better. In the quota study these are diagnostic values. Blocking ranks plays by static equity minus 1.4 times the mean best opponent placement reply score. Setup ranks plays by static equity plus 0.75 times the setup component. Raw signals adds up to five pure threat and five pure setup candidates within 25 equity points of the best static play. Exchange adds up to five exchanges within 35 points. Combined takes their union. The fresh quota experiment adds a best placement for each tile count within 35 points, plus the exchange quota.

All rollouts use no-PAT static play. Primary pool sims have a 15-second maximum and may stop early; reference sims use an independent seed and round-robin allocation. Repaired references replace seven original runs. ± values are standard errors, not confidence intervals. Gains use the static pool’s selected play evaluated in the same reference. A dash means the candidate was not evaluated in that result set. Deep references cover selected positions and the top 25 plus targeted additions, rather than the full reference pool. These are sampled simulation estimates, not measured full-game strength.
