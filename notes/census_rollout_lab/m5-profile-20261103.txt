WHY THE GATHER IS SLOW
  Each split of the rack (up to 127 for 7 tiles) makes three random loads into
  lattice-sized arrays: the root table's offsets, the candidate's offsets and
  lazy.leave. The English lattice has 4,114,349 multisets of up to 7 tiles, so
  each array is 15.7 MB: 47 MB per gather against a 16 MB L2 shared by six cores,
  and each candidate has its own 15.7 MB offsets. On a root-cache hit, the gather
  still walks crl_empty_table, a calloc'd 15.7 MB array of zeros, at random.

CANDIDATES, RANKED BY WHAT THE COUNTERS SUPPORT (none tried yet)
  1. Gather memory: skip the root table entirely on a cache hit (no empty-table
     walk); read the rack's leaves from its RIT entry instead of lazy.leave;
     index offsets by the rack's own subracks or a compact map of the nonempty
     P instead of the global rank; or compute every split's index first and
     load in a second pass, so the loads overlap instead of serializing behind
     mispredicts.
  2. crl_raise: a branch-free max. Re-run discarded_sampling to check the
     mispredicts don't just move to the loop exits.
  3. Odometer, empty-split skip and entry loops: table-driven enumeration per
     rack shape (15 count patterns for 7 tiles).
  4. Sim-start builds: 7% of this workload; parallel across candidates.
  5. Lane hashing: hash only the lanes the last move touched.

Max-ply sweep: test/census_rollout_lab_test.c's profile driver now reads
CRL_MAX_PLY (default 3). -1 builds the tables but serves nothing. Two 6-ply
sweeps in opposite orders agreed within 0.06 s.
  max_ply          -1     0     1     2     3     4     5    (plain)
  6-ply  s       9.06  8.29  7.85  7.56  7.44  7.40  7.37   8.78
  2-ply  s       7.11  5.05  3.74  3.76  3.75     -     -   6.81
Each served ply saves (6-ply): ply 0 0.77 s, ply 1 0.45, ply 2 0.29,
ply 3 0.12, ply 4 about 0.03, ply 5 about 0.03. Every ply still pays; serving
plies 4-5 too gains about 0.7%. The table builds cost about 0.3 s per four
sims whatever the depth (8% of lazy's 2-ply time, 4% at 6-ply).
Why depth hurts: the saving per served generation falls from about 9.6 us at
ply 0 to about 1.5 us at ply 3 (fewer known anchors as lanes dirty), while the
gather costs about 1.6 us every time (3.0G cycles / ~400k generations at
2-ply). So at ply 3 the gather eats about half the gross saving, and a cheaper
gather pays off most at the deep plies, where it could make serving plies
4-5 worth more.

GATHER FIXES (tried later on 2026-11-03; all exact)
=====================================================================
Each fix built alone behind a switch, against the same base (g0 = the lab
with CRL_MAX_PLY and no per-query timers), timed on the fixed profile work in
forward/reverse order, two rounds (four runs each):
                                    2-ply     6-ply
  skip empty tables (A)             -0.8%     -0.3%   (small, kept: free)
  branch-free crl_raise (B)         -4.9%     -2.1%   (kept)
  leaves from the RIT entry (C)      0.0%     -0.2%   (dropped)
  A+B+C                             -5.8%     -3.0%
  A+B (one pass)                    -6.0%     -2.3%
  A+B + two-pass gather (kept)      -7.3%     -3.4%
Lazy vs plain now: 1.99x at 2-ply (was 1.84x), 1.23x at 6-ply (was 1.19x).
Cycles (two alternating pairs, 2-ply): 16.2G -> 15.0G (-7.4% / -7.5%); the
gather + crl_raise fell from 17.6% to 13.0% of the base's cycles, about 7.1k
to 4.9k cycles per gather.

- B: crl_raise now compiles to csel. Mispredicts fell from 23,155 samples to
  20,351 (-12%); the gather's other branches fell too, so they did not move.
- C (dead end): the leave line's L1D and TLB misses went away, but TLB misses
  on the gather's entry loads rose and the total rose 17% (14.9k -> 17.4k
  samples): most likely the per-query lookup into the 1.9 GB RIT displaces the
  translations the gather needs. Timing flat; removed.
- Two-pass gather: the odometer first writes every split's played rank, kept
  rank and tile count (small tables only); then one pass loads every split's
  offsets and leave, independently; then the entry loops. The lattice-sized
  loads overlap instead of waiting behind the odometer's mispredicts.
- Verified: CRL_E2E single-thread at 2- and 6-ply (3000 it) and 10 threads at
  2,6-ply (20000 it, 28.6M anchor values): 0 differences, 0 out of bounds.

=====================================================================
RESTRUCTURE (later on 2026-11-03): THE CENSUS OUT OF THE LAB
=====================================================================
The lab file mixed the algorithm with its oracle and timing modes, behind a
global crl_active switch read by the sim. Now:
- src/impl/census_rollout.{c,h} (prefix census_rollout_): the algorithm
  alone, with no globals. A CensusRollout holds a sim's tables and options
  (census_rollout_prepare at sim start; only read by workers after); a
  CensusRolloutWorker holds a thread's caches and notices a new sim through
  a counter (no worker registry or lock). No per-query timers. Accessors for
  the lab: last served/steered, known anchors and values, root-cache counts,
  and the board geometry (lane squares, anchor cols, changed cols, zones,
  move signature). Defaults: plies 0-3, any bag with tiles, no carry.
- Sims: -scensus true|false (SimArgs.use_census_rollout), owned by the
  simmer. The census and rollout PAT do not mix: simulate() refuses
  -scensus true while the rollouts use PAT (ERROR_STATUS_SIM_CENSUS_WITH_PAT;
  set -patrollout false), and the census also steers no generation of a
  player whose PAT is on.
- random_variable.h: SimRolloutHooks / rv_sim_set_rollout_hooks, a generic
  seam for experiments (worker state, prepare, before/after candidate,
  best_move, carry_buckets). Replaces crl_active and crl_carry_buckets in
  src. Free when off: the default sim times the same as before (6.756 s
  both, 2-ply profile work).
- test/census_rollout_lab.{c,h}: the record/replay oracle, timing modes,
  worker registry, stats, and verification, through the hooks; the lazy mode
  wraps the census API. Build timing is the whole prepare (CrlLazyStats
  build_ns); the lazy mode's generation_ns includes the census. The E2E
  table lost its separate census and gather columns. CRL_NO_QUERY_TIMERS
  still compiles the lab's per-query timers out.
- Renames in src: MoveGen.lab_skip_anchor_cols -> skip_anchor_cols,
  gen_apply_lab_skips_and_floor -> gen_apply_skips_and_floor, the
  CRL_QUACKLE_PEG build switch -> QUACKLE_PEG_VALUES; the "Experimental
  (census rollout lab)" comments now say what uses each hook.
- New driver: CRL_SCENSUS=1 checks a -scensus true sim against the same sim
  without it, play by play (rollouts, exact equity and win% means, node
  count), and times both. 2-ply 50000 it: same results, 2.02x; 6-ply 20000
  it: same, 1.26x.
Checked: single-thread verified E2E counts identical to before the
restructure (370468, 379259, ...); 10-thread counts vary run to run in the
old binary too; lazy 3.339 s vs 3.343 s before; CLI with -pat CSW24:
census + rollout PAT is refused, census with rollout PAT off gives the same
sim. format.py clean; cppcheck's remaining findings are census_sim's (#939);
clang-tidy on census_rollout.c and the lab: no findings beyond the macOS
environment's (endian.h, best_move_carry.h under -D__linux__); the driver
test file's findings predate this (atoi, fflush, widening).
