CENSUS ROLLOUT LAB: HANDOFF FROM THE M4 MINI TO THE M5 MAX, 2026-11-03
Written by the Claude Code session on the M4 Mac mini, for John O'Laughlin (olaugh).

The M4 has no Xcode (Command Line Tools only) and 9 GB free, so it can't run
xctrace. The next step, profiling the lazy census path with CPU counters
(branch mispredicts first), moves to the M5 Max.

=====================================================================
WHAT THIS IS
=====================================================================
A prototype of John's idea: fill the sim rollouts' move generation from a
census of the position, for both sides and every rack, and generate plays only
where something the rollout played has dirtied the census's space.

Where it ended up (all exact: every census-steered generation picks plain
movegen's move):
- A lazy per-anchor census. At the start of each sim, before any worker runs,
  it builds per-anchor play-score tables (for every placed multiset P, the
  anchors it is played from and its best score in each) for the position and,
  for each candidate, only the anchors that candidate changes.
- Each rollout generation gathers its rack's best in every anchor from those
  tables (score + leave + pre-endgame adjustment by tiles played) and skips
  every anchor known to be worse than the best known one.
- An anchor is known when its lane is unchanged since the candidate's board,
  or when its zone (what its plays can cover or border with the rack's tiles)
  misses every changed square on both boards.
- It serves rollout plies 0-3, any bag with tiles (not the endgame), with PAT
  off in rollouts.

RUNNING IT
=====================================================================
make magpie_test BUILD=no_pgo_release, then bin/magpie_test censusrollout with:
- CRL_TIMED=1 CRL_TLIM=14 CRL_THREADS=10 CRL_RITMMAP=0 CRL_TURNS=6,14,18,22,26 CRL_PLIES=2,6
  Iterations completed in 14 s (about a game-in-3-minutes move) on all threads,
  for: carry (as shipped), plain (no carry), carry with CRL_BIG_BUCKETS
  (16384), lazy + carry, lazy + plain, and carry again as a drift control.
- CRL_E2E=1 ... CRL_IT=N [CRL_THREADS=N] [CRL_VERIFY=0] [CRL_WARMUP=0] [CRL_PLAIN=1] [CRL_LOW_BAG=0|1] [CRL_MAX_PLY=3]
  Fixed iterations: carry vs lazy wall time, and a verified run. The verified
  run checks each census-steered move against plain movegen and against the
  best of every play, and checks every gathered anchor value against every
  play.
- CRL_PROFILE=lazy|plain|carry CRL_RITMMAP=0 CRL_TURNS=14,18,22,26 CRL_PLIES=2 CRL_IT=50000
  Fixed single-threaded work for a profiler: one sim per position in one mode,
  and nothing else. On the M4: lazy 4.1 s, plain 7.5 s for 200,001
  iterations. The lazy and plain runs play the same rollouts (identical moves),
  so their totals compare.
- Default (no env), CRL_CONTROLS=1, CRL_GATHER=1, CRL_BUILD=1
  The single-threaded record/replay oracle (lane vs anchor variants, top-k
  lists), the identity, skips-only and floor-only controls, the gather
  benchmark with exactness checks, and the eager-build timing.

Always use CRL_RITMMAP=0 for timing. With an mmapped RIT and no warm-up, the
first sim run pays RIT page faults; that biased one comparison on the M4 until
it was caught.

RESULTS SO FAR (M4, CSW24, success_long_game.gcg turns 6-26, 8 candidates, PAT off in rollouts, inference off)
=====================================================================
Timed, 14 s on 10 threads, iterations vs the shipped sim (carry). The shipped
sim does about 2.2M iterations at 2-ply and 0.7M at 6-ply per move. The drift
control agreed within 1%.

  bag  2-ply: plain  lazy+carry  lazy+plain   6-ply: plain  lazy+carry  lazy+plain
   81        1.05x     1.49x       1.59x             1.05x     1.07x       1.13x
   55        1.03x     1.75x       1.86x             1.05x     1.19x       1.26x
   44        1.05x     1.96x       2.11x             1.05x     1.25x       1.34x
   32        1.06x     1.79x       1.92x             1.05x     1.19x       1.26x
   24        1.05x     1.94x       2.09x             1.04x     1.28x       1.33x

- At this scale the shipped best-move carry is about 5% SLOWER than no carry.
  Its 1024 buckets of ~12 KB thrash once racks stop recurring, and 16384
  buckets barely help. This is independent of the census: switching the carry
  off for long sims may be a free 5%.
- The lazy census does better without the carry (+7%).
- The single-threaded record/replay showed why depth hurts:
  - per-candidate census boards leave 0 dirty lanes on rollout ply 0;
  - the reply then dirties about 9 lanes, and each later move about 4 more;
  - at plies 0..5 the best move is in a known anchor 99, 47, 35, 25, 17 and 13%
    of the time.
  Per-anchor validity beats per-lane by about 10-15% at every depth.
- Exactness:
  - 0 mismatches in every verified run (single and 10 threads, about 1.8M
    replayed generations);
  - 0 of 17M gathered anchor values out of bounds.
  - With Quackle's nonzero pre-endgame values (build with
    DEPFLAGS='-MMD -MP -DCRL_QUACKLE_PEG'), the census values stay exact (0 of
    1.7M out of bounds). But plain movegen on this pre-#931 base disagrees with
    the best of every play in hundreds of generations, so verifying the chosen
    moves end to end needs a rebase onto #931.
    peg_adjust_fill_by_tiles_played there is the hook the gather wants.

What `sample` showed on the M4 (function level only, which the skill warns can
misattribute stalls). Lazy profile workload, about 5,000 main-thread samples:
- generate_moves 23%;
- crl_lazy_gather 19%;
- _platform_memmove 8%, from sim_rollout (222) and game_backup (157), which is
  the sim's own bookkeeping that the carry baseline pays too;
- sim_rollout 5%;
- the shadow functions about 10% together;
- gen_hash_game_lanes 4%;
- mach_absolute_time 2%, which is the lab's own per-query timers. Compile
  those out of the lazy path before trusting small shares.

Candidates for counters to confirm or kill. Don't fix them blind; the skill's
history shows mispredict fixes that only moved the mispredicts:
- crl_lazy_gather:
  - the split odometer's data-dependent loop exits;
  - "skip splits with no entries";
  - the variable-length per-split entry loops;
  - crl_raise's compare.
  Ideas: a table-driven enumeration per rack shape (there are 15 count
  patterns for 7 tiles), and reading the rack's leaves from its RIT entry
  instead of 127 leave[] lookups.
- crl_lazy_skips: the lane memo checks, the zones walk for changed lanes, the
  skip-mask loops.
- movegen with skip_anchor_cols: whether skipping anchors changes shadow's

=====================================================================
DEAD ENDS (WITH NUMBERS; DON'T RETRY WITHOUT A NEW IDEA)
=====================================================================
- Eager per-lane top-k tables built with #939's rack-best scatter: 0.1-2.1 s
  for the root plus up to 0.9 s per candidate, single thread. That's 150k-800k
  iterations to break even.
- A cutoff floor (the best known value as movegen's target): 0 gain. All the
  gain is in the skips.
- A top-1 lane cache: no gain; a skip needs the second value.
- Presence bitmaps in front of the gather: 0 gain, and they cost build time.
- Incremental ranks in the gather: small gain (kept).
- Caching each rack's root bests across candidates: hits 59-74% at 4k
  iterations, 13-52% at 200k (kept).
